Our story

From a small kitchen
to your sweetest memories.

Élevé began with one well-loved oven, a flour-dusted kitchen, and the belief that the best celebrations are made of little things. Founder and pastry chef Aria Mehta started baking for the people she loved; soon, those recipes were finding their way to weddings, birthdays, and ordinary Tuesdays all over Pune.

Today, we still work in small batches, still taste as we go, and still believe a beautiful bake can make a moment feel like yours.
